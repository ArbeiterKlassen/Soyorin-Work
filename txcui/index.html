About Me

I am currently a sophomore undergraduate student at the Electronic Information School, Wuhan University, working at LIESMARS (State Key Laboratory of Information Engineering in Surveying, Mapping and Remote Sensing). My academic interests lie at the intersection of computer vision, robotics, and sensor fusion — particularly visual-inertial SLAM systems and their real-world deployment. I am actively building a technical skill set across embedded systems, signal processing, and autonomous navigation.

Outside of coursework, I maintain a personal blog at soyorin.work where I document my learning journey, technical notes, and project write-ups.
